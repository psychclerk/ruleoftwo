📖 About NeuroPsych Trainer

What is the Rule of Two?

The Rule of Two is a powerful medical education mnemonic that helps physicians and medical students remember the essential clinical features of common diseases. This systematic approach emphasizes that for any significant medical condition, there are typically two key symptoms, two characteristic signs, and two definitive investigations that are most important for diagnosis.

How to Use This Tool

Learn Mode: Start by revealing the two symptoms, then test your knowledge before revealing the signs and investigations. This progressive disclosure approach helps build strong diagnostic reasoning skills.

Quiz Mode: Test your knowledge by selecting the correct diagnosis from multiple choice options. This helps reinforce learning and identify areas needing more study.

Why This Approach Works

Research in medical education shows that active recall and spaced repetition are highly effective learning strategies. By progressively revealing clinical information and testing yourself, you engage deeper cognitive processing than passive reading alone.

Features

  • Progressive Disclosure: Reveal information step by step to test your recall
  • Quiz Mode: Multiple-choice questions to test your knowledge
  • Progress Tracking: Save your progress and track completion by section
  • Favorites: Mark important cases for quick review
  • Search: Quickly find specific diagnoses or conditions

This tool is for educational purposes only. Always apply clinical judgment and follow current guidelines when making patient care decisions. Medicine is complex and individual patient presentations may vary from classic patterns.
